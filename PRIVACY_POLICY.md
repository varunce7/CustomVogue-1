## 1. Overview

CustomVogue ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a Shopify application that allows merchants to add custom content fields to their product pages. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Merchant Data

When you install the App, we collect and store:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Shopify shop domain | Identify your store and authenticate API requests |
| Shopify access token | Make authorized calls to the Shopify Admin API on your behalf |
| Billing subscription status | Determine which plan features are available to your account |

### 2.2 Product & Field Data

When you create or edit custom fields, we store:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Shopify product IDs | Associate fields with the correct products |
| Product titles | Display human-readable labels in the app interface |
| Field content (title, text, images, display style, sort order, font) | Render custom fields on your storefront |

This data is stored in our database and also synced to your store's Shopify metafields so it can be rendered on your storefront.

### 2.3 Analytics Data (Growth Plan)

If you are on the Growth plan, we additionally collect:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Field interaction events (accordion opens, tab clicks) | Power the Analytics dashboard — show which fields shoppers engage with |
| Timestamps of field creation | Calculate monthly growth metrics |

Interaction events are triggered by shopper activity on your storefront. We record **which field was interacted with and when** — we do not record any personally identifiable information about the shopper (no names, emails, IP addresses, or device fingerprints).

### 2.4 Custom CSS

If you use the Custom CSS feature (Growth plan), the CSS you write is stored in our database and injected into your storefront pages. We do not analyze or share this content.

### 2.5 Contact Form Submissions

If you use the in-app contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message content in order to respond to your support request.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to:

- Provide and operate the App's core functionality (creating, editing, and displaying custom fields)
- Sync field data to your Shopify store via metafields
- Enforce plan limits (Free: 100 products, 5 fields/product; Growth: unlimited)
- Display analytics and usage insights to you on the Analytics dashboard
- Process billing through Shopify's billing API
- Respond to support requests

We do **not**:

- Sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes
- Use your product content or field data to train AI models
- Track your shoppers beyond the field interaction events described above

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- Data is stored in a MongoDB database hosted on a secured cloud server.
- Shopify access tokens are stored encrypted at rest.
- All communication between the App and Shopify, and between your browser and our servers, is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
- We follow Shopify's Partner Program requirements for data security.

---

## 5. Data Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Merchant account data (shop domain, access token) | Retained while the App is installed. Deleted within 48 hours of uninstall upon request. |
| Product field data | Retained while the App is installed. Deleted upon request after uninstall. |
| Analytics interaction events | Retained for up to 12 months. |
| Contact form submissions | Retained for up to 6 months after the support case is resolved. |

To request deletion of your data, email **scholarjms@gmail.com** with your shop domain.

## 8. Your Rights

As a merchant using the App, you have the right to:

- **Access** — request a copy of the data we hold about your store
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion** — request deletion of all your data (see Section 5)
- **Portability** — export your field data using the CSV Export feature (Growth plan) at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at **scholarjms@gmail.com**.

## 9. Shoppers on Your Store

If you use the Analytics feature (Growth plan), the App collects anonymous interaction events when shoppers click on custom field accordions or tabs. These events contain:

- A field identifier (internal ID)
- A timestamp

No shopper personally identifiable information (name, email, IP address, location, device ID) is collected or stored by the App. You are responsible for disclosing the use of interaction tracking in your own store's privacy policy if required by applicable law (e.g., GDPR, CCPA).
